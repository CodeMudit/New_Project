1. Registration Rules
  • Each team must consist of at most 3 members.
  • The entry fee is ₹100 per team.
  • Registration must be completed before the event begins.
  • Once registered, team members cannot be changed without permission from the organisers.
2. Event Rules
  • All team members must participate actively in the event.
  • The event will consist of 4 Levels.
  • Teams must follow the instructions given by the organisers at every stage.
  • Each challenge must be completed within the specified time limit.
  • Each team must have at least one laptop among its members.
  • Any form of cheating, unfair assistance, or violation of event instructions may lead to immediate disqualification.
3. Scoring & Winners
  • Points will be awarded based on accuracy, speed, and successful completion of challenges.
  • In case of a tie, a tie-breaker challenge may be conducted.
  • The team with the highest final score will be declared the winner.
  • The organisers' decision regarding scoring and results will be final.
4. Code of Conduct
  • Participants must maintain discipline and respect for fellow participants and organisers.
  • Misbehaviour, damage to college/event property, or disruption of the event may result in disqualification.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
5. Important Instructions
  • Participants must report to the venue before the reporting time.
  • Late entry may not be permitted once a round has started.
  • Participants must carry their college ID card.
  • Organisers reserve the right to modify the rules if required for the smooth conduct of the event.
Fullscreen + tab-switch monitoring is active during rounds. Security breaches can lead to immediate termination of your session.
